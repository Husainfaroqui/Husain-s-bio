Microscopy allows scientists to investigate structures far smaller than the human eye can resolve. It connects what we observe at the visible scale with the hidden architecture of biological systems.

Microstructures

SEEING THE UNSEEN

Different tools.
Different worlds.

Different microscopy techniques allow scientists to observe different properties of matter and biological structures.

Light Microscopy

Uses visible light and lenses to magnify specimens. It is widely used for observing cells, tissues and microorganisms.

Electron Microscopy

Uses electrons instead of visible light to achieve much higher resolution and reveal extremely small structures.

Fluorescence Microscopy

Uses fluorescent molecules to highlight selected structures, making specific components easier to study.

Microscopy & Medicine

Microscopy supports medical research, pathology, microbiology and the study of disease at the cellular level.
