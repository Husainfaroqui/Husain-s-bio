01 · EXPLORING THE INVISIBLE

Beyond what the eye can see.

Microscopy transforms structures too small to observe directly into something we can study, compare and understand. It connects the visible world of biology with the microscopic organization that makes life possible.

01

Cells

The fundamental units of life. Microscopy allows us to study their structures, organization and specialized functions.

02

Tissues

Groups of specialized cells can be examined to understand how microscopic structures create organized biological systems.

03

Microstructures

Tiny biological structures reveal patterns and details that help researchers understand how living systems work.

02 · MICROSCOPIC WORLDS

Different lenses.
Different discoveries.

LIGHT

Light Microscopy

Uses visible light and lenses to magnify specimens and reveal cellular and tissue structures.

ELECTRON

Electron Microscopy

Uses electrons instead of visible light to investigate extremely small structures with much greater resolution.

FLUORESCENCE

Fluorescence

Fluorescent molecules can highlight specific structures, helping researchers distinguish biological components.

03 · MICROSCOPY & MEDICINE

From observation
to understanding.

Microscopy plays an important role in biology, medicine and scientific research. Studying cells and tissues can help reveal normal organization as well as changes associated with disease.
